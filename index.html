What makes it different

Not another repo list. A competitive layer for your codebase.

This concept treats every repository like a tournament contender. Instead of static portfolio tiles, your projects enter seeded brackets, earn scores from GitHub signals, and surface stronger narratives: momentum, maintainability, popularity, and match-by-match performance.

Repository-first gameplay

Pull in your repositories and instantly generate head-to-head rounds using stars, last activity, language, release cadence, issue counts, or your own custom scoring formula.

Auto seeding from GitHub metadata

Prioritize visibility, freshness, or technical depth depending on tournament mode.

Developer portfolio storytelling

Show how projects compare instead of forcing users to scan endless repository grids.

Live event potential

Perfect for hackathons, coding streams, community ladders, or internal engineering showcases.

Scoring inputs

Stars & visibility

Recent activity

Issue health

Measure responsiveness and backlog quality.

Custom judge votes

Bracket experience

Seed the field. Simulate the rounds. Crown the strongest repo.

Use tournament views for public-facing showcases or private internal evaluation. Each round can combine automated metrics with human voting, making the app useful for both fun competition and serious selection.
